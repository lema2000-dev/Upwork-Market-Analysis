\chapter{Introduction}

    \section{Background}

    Digital labour platforms have significantly transformed the global labour market over the past decade by 
    enabling organisations and independent professionals to collaborate across geographical boundaries. 
    These platforms provide businesses with flexible access to specialised expertise while simultaneously creating new 
    employment opportunities for freelancers worldwide. Among the different forms of platform-mediated work, online freelancing platforms 
    have become a particularly important segment of the digital labour market by connecting businesses 
    with specialised professionals worldwide \cite{oecd2023_platformwork,oecd2019_platformworkers}.

    Online freelancing platforms play a central role within the digital labour market by enabling 
    knowledge-intensive services such as software development, data analysis, graphic design and consulting
    to be delivered entirely online.The OECD identifies platforms such as Upwork, Freelancer and PeoplePerHour 
    as representative examples of online freelancing platforms \cite{oecd2023_platformwork}.

    Upwork is one of the most prominent online freelancing platforms, connecting businesses with independent professionals across
    a broad range of knowledge-intensive disciplines. Its marketplace covers fields including software development,
    data science, engineering, design, writing and consulting, making it representative of the highly skilled segment
    of digital platform work. Owing to its broad occupational coverage and global marketplace, Upwork provides a
    relevant case for examining labour market trends, skill demand project characteristics
    within the online freelancing economy \cite{oecd2023_platformwork,upwork2024_gigeconomy}.
    
    \section{Motivation}

    Although online freelancing platforms provide access to a global marketplace, entering these platforms can be challenging for newcomers. 
    Beyond possessing technical expertise, new freelancers must establish credibility in an environment where client decisions are strongly 
    influenced by previous work history, portfolio quality and platform reputation. At the same time, rapidly evolving technologies and
    changing client requirements require continuous adaptation, making it difficult for beginners to determine which skills are most valuable to
    develop and how they should position themselves within the market \cite{oecd2023_freelancers,oecd2019_skillcertificates,oecd2019_skillsoutlook}.

    Consequently, aspiring freelancers are faced with several practical questions before entering the market.
    Which technical skills are currently in demand? What types of portfolio projects best demonstrate professional competence?
    Which market segments offer realistic opportunities for beginners? How can professional credibility be established without an
    existing client history? Addressing these questions requires a systematic understanding of current market conditions rather than
    relying solely on personal assumptions or anecdotal advice \cite{oecd2023_freelancers,oxford2020_learning}.

    The present project was motivated by the need to address these challenges through a data-driven analysis of the Upwork marketplace.
    Rather than relying on subjective opinions or informal recommendations, the project investigates publicly available job marketplace to
    identify market trends, demanded skills and portfolio opportunities that may support prospective freelancers in making more informed
    decisions about their professional development.

    \section{Project Objectives}
    
    The primary objective of this project is to provide a data-driven analysis of the current Upwork marketplace within selected data-related
    freelance categories by examining publicly available marketplace data. The study focuses on areas including data analysis, data engineering,
    Python automation and machine learning in order to provide an objective overview of current market demand, required skills and project
    characteristics within this segment of the online freelancing market.

    To achieve this objective, the project involves the systematic collection, organisation and analysis of publicly available marketplace
    data from the selected Upwork categories. Particular attention is given to identifying the most frequently requested technical skills,
    analysing job budgets and pricing models, examining the distribution of job types and exploring relationships between different
    marketplace characteristics. The results are presented using descriptive statistical methods and clear visualisations in order to provide
    an accessible overview of the analysed market segment. The project is intended to provide a descriptive analysis of the current marketplace
    rather than to predict future labour market trends.

    The expected outcome of the project is a comprehensive overview of the selected segment of the Upwork marketplace based on objective,
    data-driven analysis. By identifying current demand for technical skills, common project characteristics and prevailing pricing patterns,
    the study aims to provide practical insights that may support aspiring freelancers in making more informed decisions about skill development,
    portfolio design and market positioning. Furthermore, the project demonstrates a structured workflow for collecting, processing and
    analysing online marketplace data, providing a methodological framework that can be adapted to similar marketplace analyses.

    \section{Research Questions}

    To achieve the objectives described in the previous section, the project addresses the following research questions concerning the
    selected Python-related data freelance categories on the Upwork marketplace:

    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Which types of projects are most frequently posted?
        \item Which Python libraries and frameworks are most commonly required?
        \item Which technologies and software tools are most frequently required?
        \item Which combinations of technologies are most frequently requested together?
        \item What budget ranges and hourly rates are associated with the analysed freelance jobs?
        \item Which portfolio projects would best reflect current client demand?
    \end{enumerate}

    Each research question is addressed in a dedicated section of the exploratory data analysis,
    while the final question synthesises the findings into a practical portfolio development strategy.

    \section{Document Structure}

    The remainder of this report is organised into four main phases that reflect the overall workflow of the project.
    Following this introduction, Chapter~2 presents the data collection process, including the definition of the analysed market segment,
    the data acquisition procedure and the preparation of the dataset for subsequent analysis.

    Chapter~3 contains the exploratory data analysis and is organised around the research questions introduced in the previous section.
    Each research question is addressed individually through descriptive statistical analyses and data visualisations in order to provide a
    comprehensive understanding of the selected Upwork market segment.

    Building upon these findings, Chapter~4 synthesises the analytical results into broader market insights, identifies the most valuable
    technical skills and technologies, and proposes practical recommendations for aspiring freelancers. Chapter~5 translates these findings
    into a portfolio development strategy by recommending a sequence of portfolio projects designed to maximise
    their relevance to current market demand.

    Finally, Chapter~6 summarises the main findings of the project, discusses its limitations and outlines potential directions for future work.
