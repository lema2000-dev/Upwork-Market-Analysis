\chapter{Introduction}

    \subsection{Background}

    Digital labour platforms have significantly transformed the global labour market over the past decade by 
    enabling organisations and independent professionals to collaborate across geographical boundaries. 
    These platforms provide businesses with flexible access to specialised expertise while simultaneously creating new 
    employment opportunities for freelancers worldwide. As a result, platform-mediated work has become an increasingly 
    important component of the modern digital economy, attracting growing attention from researchers, policymakers and 
    international organisations \cite{oecd2023_platformwork,oecd2019_platformworkers}.
