Summary
Seeking a Python developer/statistician to build a Monte Carlo simulator for a proprietary poker-style card game. The rules and strategy decisions are already defined. The ideal candidate will have experience in statistical analysis and Python development, with a focus on creating efficient and accurate simulations.
