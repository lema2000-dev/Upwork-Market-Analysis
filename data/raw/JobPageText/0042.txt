Project Overview
We are looking for an experienced Operations Research (OR) Developer / Constraint Programming expert to build a mathematical optimization model for our business.

We manage a network of merchants who require physical visits from our agents to replenish their working balance. Merchants consume their balance at different daily rates. The goal of this project is to build an algorithm that predicts when a merchant's balance will run out and schedules an agent visit before they hit zero, while minimizing travel and maximizing agent efficiency.

The Technical Challenge
This is a classic Inventory Routing / Field Service Scheduling problem. We need a developer who can translate our business rules into constraints using Google OR-Tools (specifically the CP-SAT solver) in Python.
