Data Analyst - WooCommerce - Product Compliance Review (AI / Excel / Data Analysis)
Posted 2 weeks ago
Worldwide

Summary
We're looking for someone to help us review product data from a WooCommerce store and identify compliance-related information.

For each product, we need to determine:

* Is a Safety Data Sheet (SDS/PDF) available?
* Does the product contain any medical or health claims?
* Does the product contain Hazard Statement **H314**?

We're looking for someone who can recommend the fastest and most accurate way to complete this review, whether that's using AI, Excel, scripts, or another reliable method.

**Requirements:**

* Experience with Excel and large datasets
* Experience using AI tools for data analysis
* Strong attention to detail
* Experience with WooCommerce product data
