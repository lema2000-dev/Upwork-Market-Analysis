Data Extraction and Analysis Specialist
Posted yesterday
Worldwide

Summary
We’re looking for a Data Scientist to help us organize and make better use of historical data across approx thousands of deals.

The data currently sits across different sources and documents, with several missing or inconsistent mappings. We need someone who can help us:

* Clean and connect the historical data
* Pull key information and lender details from deal documents
* Map deals by industry, region and other relevant fields
* Build a model that can use this historical data to predict the same information for future deals

We’re open to Random Forest or another approach you believe would work better.

Strong experience with Python, data mapping, strong SQL queries and predictive modelling is important. Experience working with financial data and extracting information from documents would be a plus.

Please share any similar projects you’ve worked on and a brief note on how you’d approach this.
