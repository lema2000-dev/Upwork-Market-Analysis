Data Analyst for Multi‑Sensor Building Thermal Dynamics
Posted 2 months ago
Worldwide

Summary
We are conducting a long‑term scientific measurement project to analyze the real thermal behavior of a residential building. The goal is to evaluate how the building reacts to heating, cooling, and environmental conditions using multi‑sensor time‑series data (temperature, humidity, heating system behavior, and external climate data).

We are looking for a skilled Time‑Series Data Analyst who can clean, structure, and analyze sensor data collected over several months. The work includes identifying patterns, detecting anomalies, calculating thermal response characteristics, and generating clear visualizations and reports.

Your responsibilities:

Clean and preprocess multi‑sensor time‑series datasets

Detect anomalies, missing values, and sensor irregularities

Perform statistical and time‑series analysis (correlations, regressions, gradients)

Analyze thermal response curves and building dynamics

Create clear visualizations (plots, heatmaps, trends)

Document findings in a structured, professional format

Provide insights that help validate the building’s real thermal performance

Required skills:

Strong experience with time‑series analysis

Python (Pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib/Seaborn/Plotly)

Data cleaning and preprocessing

Statistical modeling

Signal processing (filters, smoothing, derivatives)

Anomaly detection

Clear technical documentation and reporting

Nice to have:

Background in building physics, HVAC, or energy systems

Experience with long‑term sensor data

Understanding of thermal inertia and building envelope behavior

This is a focused, technical project with high scientific relevance. We value clear communication, clean code, and precise analytical thinking.
