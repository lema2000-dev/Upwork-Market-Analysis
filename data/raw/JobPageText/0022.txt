AI Developer (Python + Claude API) – Lab Report Automation & AI-Generated Medical Longevity Program
Posted last week
Worldwide

Summary
We're a longevity and hormone optimization medical practice looking for an experienced AI developer to build a document-automation and AI-reasoning tool.

**What this tool needs to do:**

1. **Intake** – Accept uploaded patient lab results (PDF/CSV from LabCorp, Quest, or scanned reports) and extract the relevant values.
2. **Mapping** – Match the extracted lab values to the correct blank fields in our existing Word document lab-review template, and auto-populate it.
3. **Generation** – Feed the lab data, along with our proprietary longevity and diet protocols, into the Claude API to draft a personalized longevity program and diet plan for the patient.
4. **Output** – Produce a completed Word document (filled lab review) plus a generated longevity/diet plan, ready for provider review and sign-off before it goes to the patient.

**Requirements:**
- Must be proficient in Python — this needs to integrate with our existing Python-based team/stack
- Must have hands-on experience with the Claude API (Anthropic) — we are standardized on Claude
- Document parsing and generation experience (PDF/CSV extraction, python-docx or similar)
- Comfortable working with structured clinical/medical protocols as prompt input
- HIPAA-compliant data handling required — all patient data must go through BAA-covered infrastructure, no exceptions
- Healthcare or medical software experience is a strong plus

**Scope & process:**
We have documented clinical protocols ready to serve as the reasoning framework/prompt library, so the core "intelligence" of the tool is already defined — this is primarily a build/integration project, not a from-scratch clinical design project.

This is a fixed-price MVP, targeted for delivery in 4–8 weeks. This is intended as a contract-to-hire relationship — strong performance on this initial build will lead to an ongoing part-time or full-time role supporting additional AI tools for our practice.

**To apply, please share:**
- 1–2 examples of similar AI/document-automation work you've built (Python + Claude API preferred)
- Your estimated timeline for this scope
- Confirmation of your experience with HIPAA-compliant hosting/data handling
